# Licence of the images in this folder

The 80 JPEG images under `train/` and `test/` are unmodified files from the PlantVillage
grape subset, as redistributed in the Kaggle "New Plant Diseases Dataset" (offline-augmented
copies of PlantVillage, by Samir Bhattarai). Filenames are unchanged. See `MANIFEST.md`.

They are **not** covered by the repository's MIT licence. The dataset author's Hugging Face
card (https://huggingface.co/datasets/mohanty/PlantVillage) gives the licence as
**CC BY-SA 3.0** (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/). Attribution:

- D. P. Hughes and M. Salathé. *An open access repository of images on plant health to
  enable the development of mobile disease diagnostics.* arXiv:1511.08060, 2015.
- S. P. Mohanty, D. P. Hughes and M. Salathé. *Using deep learning for image-based plant
  disease detection.* Frontiers in Plant Science 7:1419, 2016. doi:10.3389/fpls.2016.01419

The same terms apply to `figures/corruption_grid_*`, which shows one of these images.
